Mod name: Grimdepth

1. Add Armor Piercer enchant (for sword, axe, bow, croswbow, trident, spear). This enchant can have max 3 levels. Depending from level, on attack -> ignore 10%, 20% or 30% of target's armor. In case target has no armor - it wouldn't do anything. It should have enchant rarity of fortune enchantment and rarely spawn in dungeons. On successful hits - use a little bit of blue skull particle that Minecraft Vanilla uses for illagers potion.
2. Make so skeletons are more likely to spawn with a bow having either power or armor piercer enchant when underground. Scale their power with how deep their are. Starting from deepslate level - skeletons should come with both enchantments (max level 3 on both). About spawn rate of those bonus enchanted bows - let's say they start at 5% when just underground but grows up to 50% when close to bedrock. Around 25% when on deepslate level, so the closer to bedrock -> faster it grows.
3. Make zombies follow the same rarities as skeletons. Affected zombies should receive (at random) stone/iron shovel or pickaxe (make pickaxe more likely). Make tools be mostly stone when they are higher and mostly iron when on deepslate depth level. Additionally, from deepslate level - make zombies have fixed 35% chance to become zombie leaders. Zombie leaders should emit similar blue skull particles around them (like from potion effect) but only do it when player is close enough to perceive it. Zombie leaders should receive bonus 10% move speed and jump boost around level 1 so it can jump 1,5 block (jump on fences, etc.). Zombie leaders should spawn with 2 out of 4 armor pieces (random): [iron helmet, iron chestplate, chainmail leggings, chainmail boots]. Zombie leader should always hold iron sword with enchantment (random either armor piercer or sharpness, level 1, may randomize to have both). Important thing: ensure that max 2 zombie leaders are active at a time in nearby terrain - if there are 2, make spawn chances for others be temporarily 0%. Zombie leaders can only spawn as one, regular zombie can't suddenly be promoted.
4. Make underground creepers explode with random fusion time (between standard and 3x faster). Maybe instead hard coding this at mob spawn, you can roll the dice every time creeper attempts to explode (starts being angry). Add logic to those deep underground creepers to also focus on light sources (namely torches and lanterns (any kind), glowstone and lit lamp) - attacking player will still take priority but if not aggro on player, they may detect light source -> come closer and explode.
5. From deepslate level - have a 10% chance to spawn cave spider instead regular spider.
6. From deepslate level - have a 10% chance to spawn Vex instead of a bat.

==> Regarding those various spawn rules, try to calculate them in smart way, only when player has chance to interact with them, there's no reason to run those computations if player is like 300 blocks away but also don't set limit too small so it weirdly appears on their camera. Make sure it also will work properly on multiplayer servers.
